Introduction: CEPAT (Cepat Emergency Planning and Action Tool) helps BPBD officers respond to earthquakes faster and in a more structured way by monitoring official data and news.

Methods: The system pulls BMKG data, gathers RSS reports, analyzes risk, drafts alerts and action plans, and presents a dashboard with human approval.

Results: A historical earthquake demo shows the automated workflow produces situation reports, message drafts, and prioritized plans in one pipeline.

Conclusion: CEPAT speeds coordination, preserves accountability, and remains easy to operate.

Keywords: disaster response, earthquake, BPBD, dashboard, human-in-the-loop, AI.
